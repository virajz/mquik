The vehicle journey

From gate-in to gate-out — fully tracked.

Every step is captured, photographed, time-stamped, and accountable. No more "where is that car?"

1
Customer books

Online, by phone, or via the customer app. Time slot booked. Pickup arranged if needed.

2
Vehicle arrives

Number plate captured at the gate. Job card opened. T&Cs accepted on screen.

3
Digital inspection

Technician walks through a checklist on a tablet, takes photos, marks issues.

4
Estimate & approval

Estimate sent to customer on WhatsApp. Customer approves with one tap. Insurance share auto-calculated.

5
Parts sourced

Stock checked first, then vendors compared. Approved orders go out automatically.

6
Work executed

Technician time tracked per task. Customer can watch progress on the bay camera.

7
Final inspection & bill

Quality check + test drive. Invoice generated. E-Invoice with IRN ready.

8
Payment & delivery

Multi-mode payment. Gate pass issued. Vehicle out. Feedback request sent next day.
